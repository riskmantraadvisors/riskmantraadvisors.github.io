Small Finance Banks often grow faster than the infrastructure built to oversee them. The risk
function that was adequate at transition can be under-sized within a few years — just as the
institution faces closer supervisory attention and more complex products.

## Build in the right order

1. **Governance first.** A clear Risk Appetite Statement, committee structure and escalation matrix
   give every later decision a reference point.
2. **Credit and liquidity next.** These are where SFBs typically carry the most concentrated risk.
3. **Then market and operational risk** — scaling with treasury activity and branch footprint.
4. **Data and systems throughout.** Manual processes that work at small scale become the largest
   operational risk at large scale.

## Talent and independence

The CRO must have genuine independence and direct access to the Board Risk Committee. Equally
important is building a second line that understands the business well enough to challenge it
credibly.

## The pay-off

Institutions that invest early in risk infrastructure find that it supports growth rather than
slowing it — products are approved faster, capital is deployed more confidently, and supervisory
engagements are more constructive.
