Alok Gupta is a seasoned risk management professional with over 25 years of experience across the
Indian financial sector. He has a unique dual-track record of scaling risk functions for Small
Finance Banks as Chief Risk Officer, heading the independent risk function at a large private-sector
bank (IndusInd Bank), and managing complex group risk architectures for conglomerates such as Kotak —
covering banking, NBFC and capital-market entities.

His work spans Board and regulator interactions, enterprise risk management, capital planning and
regulatory engagement, bringing a CRO's lens to every RiskMantra mandate.
