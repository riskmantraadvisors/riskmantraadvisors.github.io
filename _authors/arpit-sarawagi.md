treasury risk across leading global and Indian banks — IndusInd, IDFC FIRST, UBS, DBS, Barclays and
ICICI. He is an expert in market risk, counterparty credit risk and ALM, has advised boards and
regulators, and brings deep hands-on experience with treasury risk systems, training and RBI
frameworks.

He has led marquee regulatory and market-structure initiatives and has hands-on experience in
setting up treasury systems end to end.
